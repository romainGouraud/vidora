# Getting help

- **How do I...?** Ask in [Q&A](https://github.com/romainGouraud/vidora/discussions/categories/q-a).
- **A site does not work:** [request a site](https://github.com/romainGouraud/vidora/issues/new?template=site-request.yml), after checking the [supported sites list](https://getvidora.com/supported-sites).
- **Something is broken:** [bug report](https://github.com/romainGouraud/vidora/issues/new?template=bug-report.yml).
- **Private help:** click **Report a problem** in the extension, or write to support@getvidora.com.
- **Guides:** [getvidora.com](https://getvidora.com/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=support) has step-by-step guides for Vimeo, Loom, Skool, Dailymotion and more.
